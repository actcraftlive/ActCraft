# Lyrics and chords

## Write or import lyrics {#write}

The **Lyrics** tab shows a song's lyrics; **Edit** opens the editor. Song > Import Lyrics…
reads text, ChordPro, LRC and Word (DOCX) files.

## Chords {#chords}

![The Lyrics tab with chords above the words](lyrics.png)

Chords go in square brackets right before the syllable: `[Am]Hello [F]darkness`. They show
above the words. **Transpose** and **Capo** change them for the whole song; chords can also be
hidden.

## Following the song {#follow}

A line can carry a time stamp (the editor's time button puts in where the song is now). Per
song, the lyrics jump to the playing line, glide smoothly, scroll at a steady speed, or stay put.

## The stage window {#stage-window}

**Stage window** opens the lyrics (or the sheet music) in a window of their own, large, for a
second screen or a monitor on stage.
