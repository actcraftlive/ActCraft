# Sheet music for the band

## A song's sheet music

The **Sheet music** tab shows a PDF (**Attach PDF…**) or a score (**Attach notation…**:
MusicXML, or a MIDI file). A score shows as notes and, for guitar and bass, as tablature, with
the bar that plays marked; **Follow playback** keeps it in view. On a PDF you can draw, highlight
and add notes.

**Notes from the MIDI file** writes a song's own MIDI file out as notes: each MIDI track becomes
a part (drums by the General MIDI map, piano on two staves, guitar and bass with tab). MIDI made
in a notation program works best; played-in MIDI comes out roughly.

## A sheet for each musician

1. Open **Musicians** in the Sheet music tab and add the band: Kim, Alex...
2. Type a name under **New sheet** ("Bass") and press **Add sheet**.
3. Choose who it is **For**, then attach its PDF or score. A score's sheet can show one part of it.
4. The **Sheet** list switches between the main sheet and the others.

A musician sees their own sheet, else a sheet for anyone.

## Sheets in the browser

Musicians can follow their sheet on a tablet or laptop:

1. Settings > Band: tick **Let musicians see their sheets in a browser**. The address and a code
   show there.
2. On the tablet, open that address in the browser (same network), enter the code once and pick
   your name.
3. The page shows your sheet for the song that plays and turns pages as it goes on.

Only computers on your local network get in, and only with the code.
