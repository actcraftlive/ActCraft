# Lights: presets, cues, effects and sequences

Lighting > Presets opens the window for all of this.

## Presets {#presets}

![The Presets window with two presets and a cue list](presets.png)

Set the lights (Fixtures window), then type a name and **Store preset**. Pressing a preset brings
its look back, at once or fading over **Fade in over**.

## Cue lists {#cue-lists}

A cue list runs looks one after the other:

1. **Add cue list**, then **Add cue** with a preset, its fade and, if it should go on by itself,
   **Then on after** (seconds).
2. **Go** (or Lighting > Next Cue, a pad, a foot switch) goes to the next cue.
   **Stop** puts the list back to the start, stops what its cues started and darkens its lights.
3. The list beside a cue (**Nothing more** at first) lets it also start a sequence or effect, or stop
   all of them.

A song's **Light cues** tab fires cues at bars of the song, on its lighting lane.

One cue is in control at a time. A cue that goes, from any list or the song's lane, takes over
from the one before: the other list stops going on by itself, and the sequences and effects the
cue before started stop, unless the new cue starts them too.

## Effects {#effects}

An effect moves a group of lights by itself: a pulse, a circle, a rainbow. Save a group first (**Save group**),
then pick a ready-made effect. In beats it follows the song's tempo.

## Sequences: animations for moving heads {#sequences}

A sequence is a run of looks over time:

1. **New sequence**: give it a name and **Add sequence**.
2. Move the moving head where it should go and set its colour, then **Record step**. Repeat for
   every step.
3. Per step: **Length** and **Move** (the part of the step spent moving there; the rest holds).
4. **Plays** over and over, once, or back and forth. **In beats**: lengths count beats of the song's
   click, so each beat or bar can be another look.
5. **Start**, or let a cue start it (the list beside the cue).

## All stop {#all-stop}

The red **All stop** at the top of the Presets window (also Lighting > All Stop, a pad or a foot
switch) stops every cue list, sequence and effect at once, and every fixture goes dark. Moving
heads stay where they point, so nothing swings round. Go or Start begins again.

## Blackout, freeze and blind {#blackout}

**Blackout** darkens all, **Freeze** holds everything where it is, **Blind** lets you change and
store looks without the audience seeing it.
