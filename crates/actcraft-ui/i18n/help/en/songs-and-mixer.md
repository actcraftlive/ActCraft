# Songs, tracks and the mixer

## Song details {#details}

At the top of the middle pane: title, artist, key and **tempo**. **Tap** sets the tempo by
tapping; **Detect** finds tempo and first beat from the audio (also Song > Detect Tempo and First
Beat). **First beat at** and the bar buttons place the beat grid exactly, so the click, beat-locked
lights and sheet music line up with the song.

Per song you can also set the **click** (whole song, count-in only, visual only, off), a
**count-in**, a **fade-out**, and whether the next song plays by itself.

## The mixer {#mixer}

Each track has a fader, mute and solo, pan, an EQ and the output it plays on. Song volume and
master sit at the end. Tracks can go to different outputs of your audio interface (Settings >
Audio), for example the click only to the in-ear mix.

## Regions: verse, chorus, loops {#regions}

Song > Add Region at Playhead marks a part of the song. A region can **play**, **loop**, be
**skipped** or **stop after**. A looping part repeats until you press **Leave Loop** (L, also on a
MIDI foot switch, see [remote actions](midi-and-remote#remote-actions)); the song then plays the
loop to its end and goes on.

Markers in WAV files become regions with Song > Regions from File Markers.

## Spoken cues {#spoken-cues}

ActCraft can say the song title at its start and words like "Chorus" or "End 3 2 1" before a
region, on a bus of its own (Settings > Spoken cues, and the song's Spoken cues tab).
